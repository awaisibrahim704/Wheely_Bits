General

Wheely Bits is a premium community and marketplace dedicated to automotive aesthetics, specifically focusing on rims, wraps, and tints. We connect enthusiasts with top-tier vendors and provide tools for perfect fitment and styling.

Vendors on our platform go through a rigorous vetting process. Look for the 'Verified Vendor' badge on their profile, which indicates they have met our quality, authenticity, and customer service standards.

Rim & Fitment

Use our Fitment Calculator tool. You'll need to input your vehicle's make, model, and year, along with the rim's diameter, width, offset (ET), and bolt pattern. The tool will simulate the clearance and provide a fitment rating.

Offset is the distance from the hub mounting surface to the centerline of the wheel. Positive offset means the hub is closer to the street side, common in newer cars. Negative offset pushes the wheel outwards for a deeper dish look.

Wrap & Tint

A high-quality vinyl wrap professionally installed typically lasts between 3 to 5 years, depending on environmental factors and how well it is maintained (e.g., garaged vs. parked outside).
